How the numbers work

  • Truck oil: oil interval minus miles driven since the last oil change. Yellow under 500 mi, red when it hits 0.
  • CDS trucks: the CDS runs off the van engine, so each TM hour since the oil change counts as 15 miles.
  • Legend GT and Apex 570: the truckmount has its own engine, so its oil is tracked in TM hours (every 100 hrs).
  • TM service: from the 100 / 400 / 500 / 1000 / 2000-hr charts. Yellow within 20 hrs. A bigger service includes the smaller service's items. If two services land within 20 hrs of each other, they're done together.
  • After an oil change or service, tap Log oil change / service so the countdown restarts from that day's miles and hours.
  • Everything is saved in your Google Sheet (Trucks and Log tabs).
